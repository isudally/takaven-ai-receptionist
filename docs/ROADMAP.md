# Execution roadmap

Planning target: 30 days from authorised execution with essential access. Dates are not scheduled yet. External account/carrier dependencies may change elapsed time.

| Window | Work | Exit condition |
|---|---|---|
| First step | Owner review and alignment repair | D23–D25, config/QA changes and execution plan approved — **COMPLETE (Gate 0A)** |
| Next step | Offline Retell mapping | Native mapping, route gaps and acceptance evidence plan; no push or live call |
| Days 1–3 after test authorisation | Lean core screening, phone smoke, finalist stress review | Pilot engine selected or honest no-decision report |
| Days 4–7 | Configure selected master automotive receptionist | Facts/actions/languages/handoffs working |
| Days 8–10 | Adversarial acceptance and client-like telephone QA | Master v1 frozen with no unresolved blockers |
| Days 8–12 | Intake, config, QA, handover and rollback templates | Repeatable deployment pack |
| Day 10 onward | Start prospecting and booking demos | Pipeline active; clearly label demo/pilot status |
| Days 10–14 | Telephone demo, short video, pricing and sales assets | Honest, demonstrable offer |
| Days 10–30 | Targeted Mauritius/UAE outreach | At least 10 demos; target first paid implementation |

If Phase 0 or access cannot fit the three-day selection window, move the start or report the dependency. Do not disguise an incomplete test as a finished comparison.

## Stop rules

- No more platform discovery after selection unless a genuine blocking deficiency appears.
- No custom voice infrastructure, benchmark platform, CRM or dashboard.
- No extra industry demos or outbound automation before the initial demo passes.
- No weeks spent improving test tooling; use supported platform features first.
- No continuation of technology polishing after Day 30 without commercial evidence. If nobody pays, review offer, targeting, price and sales execution.

## Business targets

Historical outreach targets: 30–50 Mauritius prospects and 50–100 UAE prospects. Qualify accounts before contact; an outreach target is not evidence of demand. Do not send outreach autonomously without explicit instructions.
